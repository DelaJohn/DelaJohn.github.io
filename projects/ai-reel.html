Generative AI Reel

A 60-second Vox-style short-form video exploring how generative AI is changing digital media. This project combines research, scriptwriting, editing, motion graphics, and visual storytelling.

Role: Research, Scriptwriting, Motion Design, Video Editing

Concept

The goal of this reel was to explain a complex topic in a short and engaging format. I focused on generative AI because it is directly impacting design, advertising, entertainment, and the way digital content is created.

Process

I planned the video using a timed storyboard and script, breaking the reel into sections such as the hook, overview, applications, benefits, limitations, real-world examples, and ethical questions. The edit uses fast pacing, bold text overlays, and simple motion to keep the information clear and engaging.

Outcome

The final reel shows my ability to research, organize information, and turn it into short-form content. It also highlights my editing, pacing, and motion design skills, which are especially useful for social media and content-focused design roles.
