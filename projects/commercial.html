Motion / Commercial

Lemon Energy Drink

This project is a short-form commercial created to promote a lemon-flavoured energy drink. The goal was to make the product feel bold, refreshing, and high-energy through motion, pacing, and composition.

Role: Motion Design, Editing, Concept Development

Concept

The idea was to create a commercial that feels quick, energetic, and visually punchy. Since the product is lemon-flavoured, I wanted the tone to feel fresh and bright while still keeping the energy drink identity strong.

Process

I focused on editing, pacing, and visual rhythm to make the commercial feel engaging in a short amount of time. The direction was built around product presence, motion, and simple but effective compositions that keep attention on the can.

Outcome

The final result is a short commercial that communicates energy and freshness clearly. This project helped me develop my motion and editing skills while thinking about how to present a product in a more dynamic way.
