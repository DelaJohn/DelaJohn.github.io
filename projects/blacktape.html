Brand Identity

black.tape

black.tape is an underground music platform identity focused on raw energy and local DJ culture across different cities. The goal was to create something that feels discovered rather than advertised, keeping it emotional, gritty, and not overly polished.

Role: Branding, Art Direction, Visual System Design

Concept

The idea behind black.tape was to build a brand that feels exclusive and rooted in underground culture. Instead of looking commercial or overly refined, the direction focused on creating something raw and slightly imperfect, similar to the atmosphere of real DJ sets and late-night events.

Process

I explored different typographic styles and developed a distorted chrome treatment to reflect energy and intensity. The system was designed to be flexible, allowing different cities to have their own presence while still maintaining a consistent structure through layout, type, and hierarchy.

Outcome

The final result is a scalable identity system that works across posters, social media, and event branding. It keeps a consistent tone while still adapting to different cities, creating a recognizable but evolving visual language.
