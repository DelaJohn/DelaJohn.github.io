Brand Identity & Social Media Campaign

50-50

A snowboard apparel brand concept built around progression, style, and rider-driven culture. The project combines visual identity design, apparel branding, and social media content to create a cohesive brand experience that resonates with freestyle snowboarders.

Role: Branding, Visual Identity, Social Media Design, Apparel Design

Concept

50-50 was built around the mindset of progression in snowboarding — constantly pushing beyond comfort zones and developing an authentic riding style. Inspired by the connection between street culture and freestyle snowboarding, the brand celebrates individuality, creativity, and self-expression both on and off the mountain.

Process

The visual identity was developed using aggressive typography, high-contrast graphics, custom iconography, and expressive patterns inspired by snowboard, skate, and street culture. Beyond the core branding system, I expanded the project into social media applications by creating Instagram post and story concepts designed to promote products, events, and seasonal campaigns while maintaining a consistent brand voice.

Outcome

The final result is a versatile brand system that extends across apparel, digital content, and marketing touchpoints. Through logo development, apparel mockups, and social media campaign concepts, the project demonstrates how a strong visual identity can create a recognizable and engaging brand experience for a niche audience.
