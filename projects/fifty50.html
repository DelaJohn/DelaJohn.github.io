Brand Identity

50-50

A snowboard apparel brand concept built around progression, style, and rider-driven culture. The project blends freestyle snowboard energy with a street-influenced identity system that feels raw, expressive, and personal.

Role: Branding, Visual Identity, Apparel Design

Concept

50-50 was built around the mindset of progression in snowboarding — constantly pushing past comfort and developing your own style. The brand reflects a street-meets-snow identity, where riders bring their personality into how they ride, not just what they wear.

Process

The visual direction focused on aggressive typography, sharp forms, and high-contrast elements inspired by skate and street culture. I explored logo variations, icons, and patterns that feel raw and energetic while still being functional across apparel and digital applications.

Outcome

The result is a bold and flexible identity system that works across clothing, graphics, and brand touchpoints. It captures the attitude of freestyle riding while allowing the brand to evolve with different riders and styles.
