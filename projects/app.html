UX/UI Design

LearnLoop

LearnLoop is a mobile app concept that combines the short-form engagement of TikTok with structured learning inspired by Duolingo. The goal was to make learning feel quick, engaging, and easy to return to.

Role: UX/UI Design, Product Thinking

Concept

The idea behind LearnLoop was to make educational content feel more engaging for people who are used to fast-paced digital platforms. Instead of treating learning like a chore, the concept was built around short, accessible content that feels easier to consume.

Process

I focused on building a simple mobile experience that feels familiar while still supporting learning. The interface was designed around clarity, engagement, and easy interaction, using short-form content and structured screens to balance entertainment with usability.

Outcome

The final result is a UI concept that shows how educational content can be presented in a more engaging and habit-forming way. This project helped me think more about user experience, interface flow, and how visual design supports digital interaction.
