Streetwear Brand

Sheesh

Sheesh is a streetwear brand concept built around bold expression and reaction. The idea was to create an identity that feels loud, immediate, and attention-grabbing, similar to the way the word itself is used when something hits instantly.

Role: Branding, Visual Identity

Concept

Sheesh was built around reaction and expression. It represents those moments where something stands out right away, whether it is a fit, a visual, or a moment in culture. The goal was to take that energy and turn it into a bold identity system.

Process

The direction focused on oversized letterforms, high contrast, and playful shapes that feel exaggerated and expressive. I explored different colorways and compositions to make sure the identity could flex across different drops while still feeling consistent.

Outcome

The final result is a streetwear concept that feels energetic and recognizable. Even though it is a simple concept project, it helped me explore how personality and attitude can be pushed through typography and graphic style.
